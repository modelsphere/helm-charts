{{- if .Values.reload.enabled }}

The autoconfig-reload sidecar watches /workspace/configs and sends SIGHUP to
cache-aware-router, which is what makes the worker list hot-reloadable.
{{- end }}

Service listens on :{{ .Values.service.port }}.

To change the configuration:

  - a controller writes the worker list into the key that
    ModelRoute.cart.outputConfigMap / outputKey point at;
  - by hand, either `kubectl edit cm {{ include "cart.configMapName" . }}`
    or edit values and `helm upgrade`.

The two do not collide, as long as the controller owns a key of its own.
